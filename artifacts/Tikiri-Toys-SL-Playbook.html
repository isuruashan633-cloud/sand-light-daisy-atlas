The whole process, in one breath

Sales and stock tell us what to make, once every three months. BOI and Customs clear it. The Operations Executive issues the purchase order only after approval and payment. Merchandising gets materials ready. Planning and Production make the goods. Shipping moves them to Wattala. Finance sets the local price. The Digital Marketing Executive puts them live and sends the daily folders. The Warehouse Assistant picks, packs and hands parcels to the courier. Then the numbers feed the next plan.

Step by step

Same order the work actually happens, with the new handovers written in.

Step 01

Forecast demand and pick the products

Owner
Risina Surandi, Data Analyst

Starts when
A local-sales range needs planning, or stock needs replenishing. Stock is topped up once every three months.

What you do

  1. 1Use Shopify sales from 2023–2026 as the main history for this work.
  2. 2Build the mix as written: 25% USA best movers, 50% Sri Lanka past best movers, and 25% Sri Lanka 2026 new launch.
  3. 3Keep the category split at 55% rubber and 45% plush.
  4. 4Calculate how many units of each product are needed for the next three months, and lock the styles.
  5. 5Present the list to Management.
  6. 6Report stock through the Power BI dashboard. A proper inventory system is still needed — Power BI is the current view, not the long-term home.

Handover

Once Management approves, Risina gives the quantity list to the Operations Executive.

Watch this. There is no separate formal Sales Planning process today. In practice, this work lives in forecasting, product selection and inventory analysis.

Step 02

Clear customs, then issue the purchase order

Owner
Isuru Asan, Operations Executive, with Shipping and Finance

Starts when
The approved quantity list is in, and the goods were made under BOI concessions for sale in Sri Lanka.

What you do

  1. 1List the stock for local sale — SKU, quantity, invoice value and any customer requirements.
  2. 2Give product details, approved selling prices, quantities and weights for Customs valuation where needed.
  3. 3Prepare the BOI Local Sale Application, Commercial Invoice, valuation details, shipping papers, and anything else BOI or Sri Lanka Customs asks for.
  4. 4Submit through the BOI approval channel. Shipping registers the CUSDEC and watches the validity window. If it expires, cancel and start again.
  5. 5Customs issues an Assessment Notice covering duty, taxes, levies and other statutory charges. Finance pays and keeps the proof.
  6. 6Only when the approval and the payment are both clear, the Operations Executive sends Merchandising a purchase order. It lists each SKU and its quantity.

Handover

The purchase order goes to the Merchandiser. Assessment and payment records stay with Finance and Shipping.

Watch this. No purchase order goes out until customs approval and payment are both clear. Goods still must not leave the zone until final clearance is on file — see Step 07 if the packed quantity changes the assessment.

Step 03

Check raw materials and packaging

Owner
Thamara Kariyawasam · backup Dhanushka Dissanayake

Starts when
The Merchandiser has the purchase order.

What you do

  1. 1Check fabrics and raw materials already in stores.
  2. 2Order or import anything that is missing — do this straight away, not later.
  3. 3At the same time, order packaging, hang tags and labels for the styles and quantities.
  4. 4Use the same global Tikiri SKU, barcode and labelling standard we use everywhere else. Local sale does not get a different code system.
  5. 5Confirm the expected ready dates for materials and packaging.
  6. 6Give the Production Planner the production order and the date the materials will be ready.

Handover

The production requirement, plus raw-material ready dates, goes to Production Planning.

Step 04

Build the production plan

Owner
Prasad, Assistant Manager — Planning · backup Krishantha

Starts when
Merchandising confirms materials and packaging are on track and sends the requirement.

What you do

  1. 1Check the requirement against real working capacity — not hoped-for capacity.
  2. 2Issue planned start and finish dates, plus the final production readiness date.
  3. 3If it is a new or revised product, include the PP sample step before full production.
  4. 4If the asked delivery date does not fit capacity, flag it before the run starts. Do not discover this mid-line.

Handover

The confirmed delivery date goes back to Merchandising. The schedule is shared with Production.

Step 05

Make the goods, check them, and finish the run

Owner
Production team with Merchandising

Starts when
The plan is approved and the materials are actually ready.

What you do

  1. 1For a new product or a design change, make a pre-production (PP) sample first.
  2. 2Merchandising checks the PP sample against the approved spec. Full production does not start without written PP approval.
  3. 3Merchandising follows the run at around 25%, 50% and 75% complete — at minimum.
  4. 4Write down and escalate any quality issue, short quantity, or date slip.
  5. 5When manufacturing is done, Planning tells Merchandising.

Handover

Merchandising hands finished goods to Finishing & Packing.

Step 06

Finish and pack the stock

Owner
Pathum Srimal · backup Shehan Rathnayake

Starts when
Manufacturing is complete and the goods are handed over for final packing.

What you do

  1. 1Pack to the merchandiser’s instructions — not to a guess.
  2. 2Complete the final packing list with carton details, CBM and gross weight.
  3. 3Confirm to the Merchandiser that packing is complete.
  4. 4Send that packing list to Shipping, and copy Merchandising.

Handover

The Merchandiser tells the Operations Executive the toys are packed. Shipping has the packing list for transport and Customs release.

Step 07

Release the goods from the zone

Owner
Shanil Costa with Sachini Madushika, on the Operations Executive’s request

Starts when
Merchandising has said the goods are packed, and Shipping has the final packing list.

What you do

  1. 1The Operations Executive asks Shipping to check clearance and arrange local transport to Wattala.
  2. 2Shipping checks the CUSDEC still matches the final quantity and weight. If the assessment changed, Finance pays the difference and keeps the proof.
  3. 3If the CUSDEC validity window has expired, cancel and start again. Do not move the goods on an expired entry.
  4. 4Submit payment evidence as required and get final clearance.
  5. 5Only after clearance and duty settlement should goods leave the zone.

Handover

Cleared goods are released for transport to the Wattala warehouse.

Watch this. Regulatory processing can take several working days after payment. Build that wait into the plan, or Wattala will sit empty while papers catch up.

Step 08

Move stock to Wattala and receive it

Owner
Operations Executive directs it. Shipping moves it. Warehouse Assistant receives it.

Starts when
Goods are cleared for local sale and ready to leave the zone.

What you do

  1. 1Shipping picks a suitable local transport using the final CBM and gross weight. The trip is from DSL Biyagama to the Wattala warehouse.
  2. 2The Colombo 10 store (273 Panchikawatta Road) is no longer the local warehouse. Do not deliver there.
  3. 3The Warehouse Assistant follows the Operations Executive’s receiving instructions. Check each SKU, quantity and condition against the transfer documents.
  4. 4Record shortages, excess units or damage separately. Keep damaged units off the saleable shelf.
  5. 5Report the actual quantities received to the Operations Executive.
  6. 6Those confirmed quantities become the opening stock in Shopify. The Digital Marketing Executive does not invent a stock figure.

Handover

Accepted stock goes into labelled locations at Wattala and can be sold in a controlled way.

Watch this. Courier collection is from Wattala. The order desk stays at Biyagama. Do not treat the factory as a second dispatch point.

Step 09

Store stock and keep the numbers honest

Owner
Warehouse Assistant, Wattala. Line manager: Sasini. Variances: Operations Executive.

Starts when
Stock has been accepted into the Wattala warehouse.

What you do

  1. 1Give every SKU a fixed, labelled shelf or rack. Keep fast movers close to packing. Keep damaged units separate.
  2. 2Pick FIFO — older stock of the same SKU goes first.
  3. 3Keep a simple SKU-to-location register so anyone on shift can find an item.
  4. 4Once inventory tracking is on, Shopify deducts sales. Receipts, dispatches and approved damage or loss still need a written reason before a number is changed.
  5. 5Flag low-stock items to Risina before we stock out.
  6. 6Once a month, count the physical units of every SKU and compare each count with the Shopify balance for Wattala.
  7. 7Recount differences, check receipts and dispatch records, and report unresolved variances to the Operations Executive. Keep the count sheet.

Handover

Accurate available stock feeds Finance, Shopify, marketing and the next three-month plan.

Step 10

Build the local cost and the B2C selling price

Owner
Sachini Madushika · costing backup Malsha Hiruni

Starts when
Products and operating costs for local sale need a price.

What you do

  1. 1DSL costing uses consumption from CAD and SMV from Industrial Engineering (Amal). Accessory specs come from the sample room where that applies.
  2. 2Include transport from DSL Biyagama to the Wattala warehouse.
  3. 3DSL manufacturing cost plus DSL profit margin gives the FOB price. DSL usually gives FOB in USD. Finance converts to LKR at the applicable rate.
  4. 4For the local B2C price, fold in CUSDEC duty, packaging, VAT, and the website payment-processing charge (noted as 3%).
  5. 5Watch the Customs codes that show up: COM, EXM, CID, PAL, SSCL and VAT.
  6. 6Tikiri does not take an extra profit on this local process. Until a new bank account is open, project costs are expected to be funded from the DSL account.

Handover

Finance issues the approved B2C selling price for each SKU.

Watch this. There is a local marketing advertising budget of LKR 300,000, expected to be paid on Ms. Gayara’s credit card, with Sachini processing it.

Step 11

Put the products live on Shopify

Owner
Digital Marketing Executive · supervised by Sasini

Starts when
Finance has the approved B2C price and Wattala stock is actually ready.

What you do

  1. 1Upload the approved SKU, description, images and final price.
  2. 2Set opening inventory from the Warehouse Assistant’s confirmed receipt. Mark anything outside the approved list as out of stock.
  3. 3Drop products into the right Shopify collections.
  4. 4Set shipping and checkout with the correct courier charges.
  5. 5Check stock, landing pages and discount codes before any promotion goes live.
  6. 6Sync the catalogue with linked social platforms so listings stay the same everywhere.
  7. 7When tracking is set up, check product views, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase events in Google Tag Manager, GA4 and the ad pixels.

Handover

A live storefront with the right products, prices and availability. Orders can now start.

Step 12

Turn marketing on — only when we can fulfil

Owner
Digital Marketing Executive for campaigns. Sasini for local marketing, and as backup.

Starts when
Wattala can dispatch, and Shopify is live with products we can actually ship.

What you do

  1. 1Plan, run and optimise Meta, Google and TikTok campaigns. Manage budgets, audiences, bidding and remarketing. Watch cost per acquisition and return on ad spend.
  2. 2Run organic posts on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest.
  3. 3List approved products on Daraz and Kapruka only with their own price lists. Do not assume Shopify will push them across.
  4. 4Plan influencers, email or SMS, and the right product-launch events. Sasini keeps the calendar of Sri Lankan exhibitions and fairs.
  5. 5Prepare weekly and monthly reports on spend, traffic, sales, conversion and acquisition cost, and say what should change.

Handover

Traffic and orders start flowing into the daily dispatch routine.

Watch this. Do not turn paid campaigns on until Wattala can pick, pack and hand parcels to the courier the same working day.

Step 13

Send the day’s orders and dispatch them

Owner
Digital Marketing Executive at Biyagama, Warehouse Assistant at Wattala, then the courier

Starts when
A customer places a fully paid order on tikiritoys.lk.

What you do

  1. 1The order lands as pending fulfilment. Nothing is picked until payment is confirmed. Pending, failed or flagged payments stay out of the list.
  2. 2Before 12:30 p.m., the Digital Marketing Executive reviews incoming Shopify orders: SKU, quantity, customer contact, delivery address and payment status. Incomplete orders are resolved or flagged.
  3. 3At 12:30 p.m., email the day’s order list to the Wattala Warehouse Assistant.
  4. 4Create one waybill per order in the courier account. Send one folder per order, containing the order details, the customer details, and the matching waybill.
  5. 5The Warehouse Assistant matches each order to its folder, picks and checks the SKU, quantity and condition, packs it, and attaches the matching waybill to the carton.
  6. 6Hold any parcel with a missing waybill, an address mismatch or a stock shortage. Tell the Digital Marketing Executive the same day. Do not hand that parcel to the courier.
  7. 7Record each order number and waybill the courier accepts. Send the courier handover documents to Biyagama before 5:00 p.m. Name any parcel still at Wattala, and why.
  8. 8When those documents arrive, the Digital Marketing Executive marks only the handed-over orders as fulfilled in Shopify. Unconfirmed orders stay open.

Handover

Tracking and a true fulfilment status sit with the Digital Marketing Executive for customer updates.

Watch this. If the Digital Marketing Executive is not available before 12:30 p.m., escalate to Sasini. Fulfilment does not go ahead without the order list and a matching waybill. Only orders actually handed to the courier are marked fulfilled.

Step 14

Follow delivery, handle complaints, and start again

Owner
Digital Marketing Executive for updates. Operations for escalation. Finance for refunds.

Starts when
An order is dispatched, a delivery issue comes in, or stock movement says we need more.

What you do

  1. 1Use the courier tracking number to check delivery. The Digital Marketing Executive updates the customer, including when Wattala reports a delay, a shortage or a missed collection.
  2. 2Customers currently reach us on mobile / WhatsApp 0773617771 and the Facebook / Instagram inbox. A dedicated customer-service email still needs to be created.
  3. 3Log every complaint before you act: date, channel, customer, contact, order number, category, description and status.
  4. 4Acknowledge quickly, gather evidence, categorise, then resolve or escalate. Safety concerns, repeat complaints, and refunds above the agreed threshold must be escalated.
  5. 5For a refund: Operations checks internally, Ms. Gayara and Mr. Dhevin approve, Operations instructs Executive Finance with Marketing copied, Finance verifies the bank and pays, Marketing tells the customer.
  6. 6Close the log with the resolution and the closing date. Produce a simple monthly summary by category and channel.
  7. 7Stock, sales and low-stock signals go back to Risina and Merchandising. The three-month cycle starts again at Step 01.

Handover

The operation returns to Step 01 whenever stock or demand needs another production run.

Watch this. Birth-gift delays and rubber-item patches keep showing up as complaint themes — pay extra attention there. Birthday-gift special wishes are noted as a future service, not a live one.

Packing for courier handover

01

Pack for real courier handling

Protect for stacking, drops and bike delivery — not only for how it looks on a shelf.

02

Right-size the parcel

Oversized boxes inflate volumetric weight and courier cost. Use the smallest box that is still safe.

03

One order, one parcel where you can

Consolidate multi-item orders. If you must split, label clearly as 1 of 2 / 2 of 2 against the same order.

04

Seal every seam

Strong tape on open edges, so the parcel cannot open or look tampered with.

05

Protect the waybill

Place it flat on the top face. Never across a seam or a corner. It must match the folder for that order.

06

Weigh and measure

Record actual weight and dimensions before handover so we can check courier billing later.

07

Include a packing slip

The slip inside must match what is physically in the parcel.

08

Final check

Parcel condition, readable label, logged weight — then the courier can take it.

Customer care, complaints and refunds

Every complaint is logged, acknowledged, handled the same way, and escalated when the first person should not decide alone.

Damaged or defective

Ask for a photo, confirm the issue, then replace or refund.

Wrong item delivered

Send the correct item and arrange return of the wrong one where needed.

Missing item

Check the dispatch log, then send the missing piece or refund that value.

Late or failed delivery

Check courier tracking, chase the courier, and give the customer a new ETA.

Quality / not as expected

Compare against the product spec and resolve under the return approach.

Safety concern or child injury risk

Escalate immediately, whatever channel it came in on. Do not sit on this.

  1. 1Log it first in one central record — before anyone starts fixing it.
  2. 2Acknowledge on the same working day if you can.
  3. 3Categorise the issue so the right path is used.
  4. 4Collect the order number, photo, or other evidence needed to decide.
  5. 5Fix first-level issues with a replacement, refund, redispatch, or a clear explanation.
  6. 6Escalate safety concerns, repeat complaints, and higher-value refunds.
  7. 7Close the loop on the same channel the customer used, and record the closing date.
  8. 8Report volumes monthly by channel and category.

The stock loop

Once goods are in and the records are updated, we watch sales, then do it again. In a peak campaign the loop should move faster than the three-month cycle.

4 · Make, move, update

PP approval where needed, production milestones, release from the zone, and a Wattala receipt that updates Shopify.
